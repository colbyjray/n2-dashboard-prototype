## Top Headlines

### 1. xAI-Anthropic Compute Deal Signals New AI Business Model
SpaceX IPO filings revealed that xAI has agreed to provide Anthropic large-scale AI compute through its Colossus and Colossus II clusters through May 2029. The deal is valued at roughly $1.25 billion per month, signaling that AI compute infrastructure is becoming a standalone monetizable business rather than just an internal capability.
- https://www.networkworld.com/article/4176194/xai-anthropic-deal-signals-the-rise-of-ai-compute-as-a-standalone-business.html

### 2. Google I/O 2026: 100+ AI Updates Including Gemini 3.5 Flash, Omni, and Spark
Google announced more than 100 AI updates at I/O. Key releases: Gemini 3.5 Flash (faster than 3.1 Pro on benchmarks, optimized for agentic workflows), Gemini Omni (multimodal model for text/video/drawings), and Gemini Spark (always-on personal agent running 24/7 in Google Cloud). Google also dropped the Ultra plan from $250 to $200, matching ChatGPT Pro and Claude Max pricing.
- https://www.youreverydayai.com/ep-784-googles-big-i-o-releases-popes-warning-on-ai-metas-dark-ai-cuts-and-more/

### 3. OpenAI Codex Ships Major Updates: Goal Mode Goes Fully Available
OpenAI released several Codex upgrades. Appshots (macOS: press both Command keys to send any app window to Codex), Goal Mode (hand Codex an open-ended objective and it works autonomously for hours/days - first widely available goal mode from a major AI company), desktop app control after Mac is locked, and plugin sharing for teams.
- https://www.youreverydayai.com/ep-784-googles-big-i-o-releases-popes-warning-on-ai-metas-dark-ai-cuts-and-more/

### 4. Grok Build CLI Launches in Beta
xAI launched Grok Build, a new coding agent and CLI, in beta for SuperGrok and X Premium Plus subscribers. It supports complex coding projects with plan mode reviews, headless mode for automation, and specialized subagents for parallel processing.

### 5. New Grok Model Finished Training
xAI says its next Grok model just finished training with 1.5T parameters, with Cursor data in the training mix. Public release expected in 2 to 3 weeks.

### 6. GPT-5.6 Leaks: Coming in June
GPT-5.6 appears heavily focused on stronger multi-step reasoning, with release expected in June.

### 7. Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus Solves Decades-Old Math Problems
AlphaProof Nexus autonomously solved 9 out of 353 open Erdos problems, including questions unanswered for decades, at inference costs of just a few hundred dollars per problem. It uses Lean to verify every step, rejecting hand-wavy logic and hallucinated lemmas.

### 8. Meta Surveilled Workers, Built AI Replacements, Fired 8,000
Meta reportedly surveilled thousands of workers for months, learned everything they do, built AI to replace them, and then fired 8,000 of them.

### 9. Elon Musk vs. OpenAI Lawsuit Dismissed
A federal jury unanimously ruled that Musk waited too long to sue Altman, Brockman, and OpenAI. The jury deliberated for less than two hours. The core claim that Altman abandoned a charitable mission for profit was never tested in court. Musk's team plans to appeal.

### 10. David Sacks Killed White House AI Executive Order with One Phone Call
The Trump administration had a major AI executive order ready to sign that would have created federal oversight for frontier AI models with NSA review. David Sacks called the president directly Thursday morning and derailed the entire thing, arguing a voluntary review process could eventually become mandatory.

## AI Industry and Business

### Sam Altman: AI Job Apocalypse "Not Likely"
OpenAI's CEO said AI has not triggered the white-collar job losses he once feared, and a full-blown "jobs apocalypse" now looks unlikely. He said the human side of many jobs still matters too much for AI to fully replace.
- https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/openais-altman-says-ai-unlikely-lead-jobs-apocalypse-2026-05-26/

### Goldman Sachs CEO: AI Job Disruption Overblown
The CEO argues AI will disrupt the job market but the US has a long track record of creating new jobs in response to disruption. The growing demand for data centers alone has created tens of thousands of new roles.

### Cloudflare Layoffs Targeted "Measurers" - AI Collapsing Coordination Overhead
Cloudflare's first mass layoff targeted coordination-heavy roles. AI can now collapse this overhead while preserving real product complexity. Companies are moving toward smaller teams of producers, with AI increasing the output gap between top and average performers.
- https://www.hackyexperiments.com/blog/the-revenge-of-the-measurers

### China Tightens AI Talent Travel Restrictions
China is restricting overseas travel for top AI professionals at companies like Alibaba and DeepSeek. Rules require approval before travel for people in advanced AI roles including founders, researchers, and senior executives.

### Kore.ai Launches AI-Native Platform for Enterprise Multiagent Systems
The Artemis edition introduces Agent Blueprint Language (ABL), an AI agent architect, and dual-brain architecture for building, governing, and optimizing enterprise multiagent systems.
- https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/05/22/kore-ai-unveils-ai-native-platform-for-enterprise-multiagent-systems/

### AI Compute Hardware: The Memory Problem
The AI hardware market is becoming a stack of memory problems. Hardware changes slowly while software and model architectures move quickly. Hardware companies will need architectures that remain useful as bottlenecks shift.

### Qualcomm Lands ByteDance AI Chip Deal
ByteDance agreed to buy millions of Qualcomm's AI chips for data centers to power AI agent software. Gives Qualcomm a foothold beyond smartphone chips into AI infrastructure.
