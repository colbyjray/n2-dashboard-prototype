## The Big Stories

### 1. Anthropic's Fable 5 Saga Continues
The biggest ongoing story in AI right now. Anthropic released what many called the most powerful AI model in the world (Fable 5 and Mythos 5), only for the U.S. government to impose export controls and force a global shutdown within days, citing bio and cyber security concerns. Key developments yesterday:
- Anthropic says access could return "within days" - executives told reporters in Seoul they're working to restore access
- Anthropic and DeepMind CEOs pushed for U.S.-led AI rules at the G7 summit
- Trump met with AI CEOs at G7 in Evian-les-Bains, with Fable 5 export controls as backdrop
- Security experts call the shutdown reckless, arguing defenders need these models to find and patch vulnerabilities
- G7 leaders are weighing "trusted partner" access to top U.S. AI models
- Elon Musk predicts a Chinese "Fable-class" model by Q1 2027, with Z.ai publicly accepting the challenge
- The Korea angle: a Korean telecom firm's reported access to Claude Mythos helped trigger Washington's scrutiny over potential China-linked risks
- Midjourney unexpectedly announces it's building a medical ultrasound body scanner, pivoting beyond image generation

### 2. Major Talent Shakeup: Google's Gemini Co-Lead Leaves for OpenAI
Noam Shazeer, Google VP of Engineering and co-lead of Gemini, announced he's leaving to join IPO-bound OpenAI. Google reportedly spent 2.7 billion dollars less than two years ago to bring Shazeer and his Character.AI team back. This is a massive talent acquisition for OpenAI and a significant blow to Google's Gemini efforts.

### 3. ChatGPT Market Share Drops Below 50% for First Time
Users are migrating to Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, and xAI Grok. ChatGPT was the fastest app to ever reach a billion monthly users and remains the most popular AI assistant worldwide, but users are increasingly willing to switch between assistants. This marks a significant shift in the AI assistant landscape.

### 4. SpaceX Acquiring Cursor for 60 Billion Dollars
SpaceX is buying the AI coding startup Cursor for 60 billion dollars in stock. Cursor crossed 1 billion dollars in annualized revenue in November 2025. The merger is expected to close in Q3 2026. Meanwhile, at its Compile conference, Cursor unveiled a 1.5 trillion-plus parameter model being trained from scratch and Origin, a Git-compatible platform built for agent-scale development, positioning it as a GitHub competitor for the AI era.

### 5. DeepSeek Becomes China's Most Valuable AI Startup
DeepSeek raised more than 7.4 billion dollars in a funding round that valued the company at more than 50 billion dollars. Founder Liang Wenfeng personally invested around 3 billion dollars. Plans to use capital for R&D expansion and computing infrastructure. A government-backed fund invested around 150 million dollars.

## AI Industry and Business Trends

- Meta has been dismantling its historically strong engineering culture by aggressively prioritizing AI initiatives. Engineers are losing the move-fast-and-break-things empowerment they had for two decades.
- Half of U.S. adults now use AI chatbots according to Pew Research, but most think AI is moving too fast and will make their data less secure.
- OpenAI is losing billions of dollars a year according to leaked financial documents.
- Adobe executives are being sued by investors who claim they made false statements about AI not using copyrighted material, costing billions in market value.
- A study on agentic coding shows a clear division of labor: humans make the majority of planning decisions while AI handles technical execution. Success depends more on domain expertise than formal programming skills. Recent trends show a measurable increase in the estimated economic value of tasks being completed.
- Enterprise AI has a runtime problem, not a model problem according to VentureBeat analysis. Runtime governance, observability, and execution control are the real bottlenecks for production AI agents.
- AI agents are putting enterprise cybersecurity frameworks to the test, forcing IT teams to rethink identity, access, governance, and runtime controls.
- OpenAI described Deployment Simulation, a pre-release evaluation method that replays real conversation contexts with candidate models to estimate deployment-time behavior.
- LLM reasoning study finds that higher reasoning effort and even later models are not always better for triaging security results.
