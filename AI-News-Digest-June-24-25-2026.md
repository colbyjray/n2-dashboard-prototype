## HEADLINE STORIES

### OpenAI and Broadcom Unveil "Jalapeño" — Their First Custom AI Chip
OpenAI and Broadcom unveiled Jalapeño, the first accelerator in a planned family of LLM inference chips optimized for performance per watt. The chip was designed in just nine months with AI-assisted development and is intended for gigawatt-scale data center deployments. This is a major step in OpenAI's plan to build the full stack behind its models and products — moving from software into custom chip hardware.
- https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/

### Anthropic Launches Claude Tag — An Always-On AI Coworker in Slack
Anthropic is retiring its existing Claude in Slack connector in favor of Claude Tag, an always-on, agentic Slack coworker. Claude Tag can join Slack organizations as a shared team member, learn from channel context and permitted data sources, and be delegated tasks via @Claude. It supports ambient updates and scheduled multi-hour or multi-day work. Anthropic says the system has become a core part of its own internal operations, with its product team using it to generate much of their code and assist with analytics, support, and debugging tasks.
- https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/06/23/anthropic-reimagines-claude-in-slack-as-nosy-always-on-agentic-ai-coworker/5260422

### Google Adds Native Computer Use to Gemini 3.5 Flash
Google launched native computer-use capabilities for Gemini 3.5 Flash, allowing the lightweight model to interact directly with digital desktop interfaces. The model processes continuous screenshots to execute click, scroll, and typing actions seamlessly across varied software environments. This brings Google's computer-use capabilities into its smaller, faster model tier.

### Anthropic's Fable 5 Status: Still on Hold, White House Talks Continue
Fable 5 is showing up in some UIs again, and some users claim they have video proof of it being used, but Anthropic says it's still serving zero traffic — the comeback may just be a UI glitch. Separately, Claude Code v2.2.190 introduces string changes hinting at preparations for Fable 5's return. Anthropic's White House negotiations are reportedly back on track after co-founder Tom Brown replaced CEO Dario Amodei (who was reportedly called a "weirdo" by White House officials) in the discussions.
- Fable 5 has also reportedly reappeared in Amazon Bedrock

## MAJOR DEALS & PARTNERSHIPS

### SpaceX Inks $6.3B Compute Deal with Reflection AI
Reflection AI is buying access to Nvidia GB300 infrastructure from SpaceX's Colossus 2 data center in a deal worth up to $6.3 billion. The agreement shows how AI labs are increasingly locking down long-term compute outside the traditional hyperscaler market.
- https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/22/spacex-inks-compute-deal-with-reflection-ai-an-open-source-ai-lab

### Qualcomm Buys Modular for $3.9B to Challenge Nvidia's CUDA Moat
Qualcomm is buying Modular for about $3.9B to strengthen its AI software layer across data centers, edge environments, and heterogeneous hardware. Modular gives Qualcomm a better shot at competing with Nvidia's CUDA ecosystem by helping AI workloads run across different chips without rewriting software for every processor.
- https://www.networkworld.com/article/4189098/qualcomms-3-9-billion-purchase-of-modular-aims-to-change-the-data-center-dynamic.html

### Micron and Anthropic Sign Multi-Year Strategic Agreement
Micron and Anthropic signed a strategic, multi-year agreement covering memory and storage supply for Anthropic data centers running Claude models, plus joint work on memory/storage architecture for AI workloads. Micron will also use Claude internally and take a stake in Anthropic's Series H funding round.
- https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-agreement-scale-next

### Superhuman (Formerly Grammarly) Acquires GPTZero
Superhuman is acquiring GPTZero to add AI detection, plagiarism checks, citation verification, source validation, and writing-origin tools into its productivity platform.
- https://siliconangle.com/2026/06/24/grammarly-parent-superhuman-buys-ai-detector-gptzero/

### Anthropic and Alibaba Launch Joint AI Model Distillation Campaign
Anthropic and Alibaba have initiated a collaborative open-source framework focused on distilling advanced reasoning intelligence from frontier models into hyper-efficient edge models. The partnership leverages Anthropic's safety-alignment techniques alongside Alibaba's massive cloud infrastructure.

### MGX Raising $50 Billion AI Fund
MGX just lined up a $50 billion raise, setting the stage for one of the biggest AI funds ever assembled.

## AI BUSINESS & STRATEGY INSIGHTS

### Gartner: AI Coding Costs Could Outrun Developer Salaries by 2028
Gartner says AI coding could become more expensive than the average developer salary by 2028 as token-based pricing overtakes flat subscriptions and usage grows. The takeaway: AI needs FinOps-style governance — usage tracking, ownership, throttling, and clear business value.
- https://www.ciodive.com/news/ai-spending-outpacing-human-developers/823690/

### Data Lakehouses Becoming the Foundation for Enterprise AI
Data lakehouses are becoming the enterprise AI foundation because they combine broad data access with governance, security, auditability, and structure. Agentic AI makes the data layer much more strategic: companies need semantic context, agent identities, access controls, and audit trails.
- https://www.cio.com/article/4184051/data-lakehouses-are-becoming-foundations-for-enterprise-ai.html

### AI Adoption Correlates with Security Incident Frequency
A survey found organizations using AI more heavily are also seeing more AI-related security incidents, reinforcing the need for access controls, monitoring, and governance before AI tools spread across workflows.
- https://www.ciodive.com/news/ai-cybersecurity-incidents-governance-jamf/823140/

### Microsoft Pushes Agentic Observability for Cloud Operations
Microsoft says cloud operations are moving from dashboards and alerts toward AI agents that reason over telemetry, incidents, and operational context — agentic observability.
- https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/23/rethinking-cloud-operations-with-agentic-observability/

### US Presses Meta to Agree to AI Model Reviews
The Trump administration is pressing Meta to submit its AI models for voluntary review. Meta is the only major US AI developer that hasn't reached such an agreement with the federal government.
